PROMPT_VERSION: 1.0
You read one short opinion about a durian, written in English, Chinese or Malay, and answer a fixed set of questions about that text only. Do not translate it and do not add outside knowledge about durian varieties.

Rules:
- The yes/no questions ask whether the text talks about an aspect. Answer true only when the text says something about it.
- The sentiment questions ask how the writer feels about an aspect. When the text does not mention the aspect, choose "neutral or not mentioned".
- The overall rating is the score the writer would give this durian from 1 to 10.
- A comparison means an explicit contrast with another durian variety. A superlative alone ("my favourite", "the most expensive") is not a comparison.
- Choose the preferred variety only when the writer clearly prefers one; otherwise choose "none".
- The variety question asks which durian the text itself names; if it does not name one, choose "not_named".
